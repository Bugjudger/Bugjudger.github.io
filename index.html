Research

I am interested in video understanding, world models, and multimodal agents. Much of my research explores how models can remember, reason about, and recognize people and events in videos. Selected papers are highlighted.

Recurrent Vision Language Models Solve the Shell Game
Dingqiang Ye, Jiacong Xu, Wen Zhang, Yiqun Mei, Vishal M. Patel

arXiv, 2026
Paper

Recurrent attention lets VLMs track hidden objects through long videos and generalize to shell games longer than those seen during training.

Identity World Model: Recognizing People in an Imagined Latent World
Dingqiang Ye, Kartik Narayan, Vishal M. Patel

arXiv, 2026
Paper

Imagining how a person would look under different conditions in latent space makes recognition more robust, without generating images.

PuppetGait: Generalizing Gait Recognition via 3D Body-Aligned LVM Features
Chengwei Ye, Dingqiang Ye, Chuanfu Shen, Zirui Zhou, Chao Fan, Shuaijun Jiang, Vishal M. Patel, Shiqi Yu

NeurIPS, 2026
Paper / Code

Aligning large vision model features to a 3D body representation improves gait recognition across changes in viewpoint and pose.

VideoResearcher: Self-Improving Tool Design for Long-Video Understanding
Dingqiang Ye*, Dongdi Zhao*, Kaishen Wang, Qingqiao Hu, Jingchen Sun, Yijun Liang, Yuqi Jia, Yiqiao Huang, Yunjie Tian, Jiaxing Zhang, Chuanyang Jin, Ke Zhang, Vishal M. Patel, Di Fu

arXiv, 2026
Paper

Video agents design, test, and refine their own tools to answer questions about long videos, without updating model weights.

Where to Look Matters: On-Policy Self-Distillation for Long-Video Understanding
Kaishen Wang, Dongdi Zhao, Yijun Liang, Dingqiang Ye, Ruibo Chen, Heng Huang, Di Fu

arXiv, 2026
Paper

A teacher that sees relevant video clues guides a full-video student, improving long-video understanding without answer labels.

InstantHDR: Single-forward Gaussian Splatting Initialization for HDR 3D Reconstruction

ECCV, 2026
Paper / Code / Dataset
